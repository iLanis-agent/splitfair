SplitFair

Two people, a pile of shared stuff, no fight. Each of you secretly spreads 100 points over the items. SplitFair hands out the items so you both end up with the same total value, and neither would swap.

Open the splitter
  • Uses the Adjusted Winner procedure (Brams and Taylor, 1996).
  • Equal for both, at least 50 of 100 each, at most one item shared.
  • Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
Not legal advice. Items split "fractionally" must be sold or shared in real life.
